01 / About Aaron Spivak

Scientific training and investment experience, brought together.

Aaron Spivak is a biotechnology investor with a background in computational and systems biology. His experience spans public and private biotechnology investing, healthcare equity research, and drug discovery research. He works as a Senior Analyst at Balyasny Asset Management, which he joined in 2025 after serving as a Principal at Great Point Partners.

Earlier in his career, Aaron worked at JFL Capital Management and Palkon Capital Management. He began his professional career as a Scientific Investigator in Computational Biology at GlaxoSmithKline. His research has spanned computational genomics, combinatorial gene regulation, antibiotic resistance, and the application of quantitative methods to preclinical and clinical drug development.
